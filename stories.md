# ⭐ My stories

STAR stories I can use in job interviews. Copy your best ones here from your retro notes.

> [!TIP]
> Use "I", not "we". Interviewers want to know what **you** did.

## Teamwork
_Tell me about a time you worked effectively in a team._

## Disagreement
_Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate._

## Mistakes and learning
_Describe a mistake you made and what you learned._

## Difficult bugs
_Tell me about a difficult bug and how you solved it._

## Pressure
_Tell me about a time you were under pressure._

## Initiative
_Tell me about a time you took initiative._

## Improving a process
_Tell me about a time you improved a process._

## Using AI
_How do you use AI tools when you code?_
